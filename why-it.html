Motivation text

When I chose to study ICT, I honestly did not know exactly what career path I wanted to follow. I had already attended a computer vocational high school, so I knew I enjoyed working with technology, but I was still unsure about what I wanted to do in the future. What made ICT interesting to me was how many different directions it could take me. I realised that IT is not limited to working for technology companies. I could use my skills in industries that interest me, such as healthcare, finance, education, or many others. That flexibility made me feel like I did not have to decide my entire future at once. Now, I want to use my time at HZ to explore different areas, build my technical skills, and discover what I enjoy most. My goal is to find a field where I can combine technology with something meaningful to me.
