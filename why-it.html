How

I’m building my programming, data, and problem-solving skills through my studies, projects and future internships. I want to explore different areas of ICT instead of deciding too early on one path.

what

Data Scientist / AI Professional

I want to use technology, data and problem-solving to find useful solutions to real-world problems.

I didn’t choose ICT because I already knew exactly what I wanted to do. What I liked about it was how many possibilities it gives me. ICT is connected to almost every industry, so I can explore different areas and see what I enjoy before deciding where I want to go. I’ve also noticed how much technology can make everyday things easier. A lot of tasks that are normally complicated, repetitive, or take a lot of time can be made simpler with the right technology. I like that idea, and I’d like to eventually create things that can make those kinds of tasks easier for other people too. I also think ICT fits quite well with how I am. I like keeping things organised, I’m pretty patient when something takes time to figure out, and I’m always curious about new things. Since technology is constantly changing, there’s always something new to learn, which I actually enjoy. For now, I’m building my programming, data, and problem-solving skills through HBO-ICT. I want to learn through projects and internships and try different areas of ICT along the way. At the moment, I’m especially interested in data and AI, so I could see myself becoming a Data Scientist or working in an AI-related field. But I’m still figuring it out, and I think that’s okay.
