My ICT Journey

My motivation, my plan, and my future in technology.

My Motivation

My interest in ICT started during three years at a vocational high school specializing in IT, where I learned the basics of programming and software development.

During a school trip to Nissan's car factory in Japan, I was fascinated by the robotic systems, especially the AI that inspects car parts. Seeing technology applied in manufacturing showed me how broad the IT field really is.

I became curious about how programming, robotics, AI, and data work together to solve real-world problems. A Python course during the summer holidays strengthened my curiosity and motivated me to study HBO-ICT at HZ University of Applied Sciences.

My Plan

During my studies at HZ, I want to build a strong foundation in programming, mathematics, and problem-solving. I will keep improving my Python skills by coding regularly and learning how to analyze and interpret data.

I also want to explore AI and machine learning through personal projects and practical assignments, such as building a small prediction model using a public dataset.

By making use of HZ's learning opportunities and gaining experience through internships, I hope to understand how these technologies are applied in professional environments.

My Future

In four years, I aim to graduate from HZ and start my career as a junior data scientist at an international company in Europe. I want to gain practical experience by analyzing real datasets and developing simple prediction models.

My long-term goal is to use data and AI to solve meaningful problems and build intelligent solutions that make technology more useful and accessible to people from all backgrounds.
