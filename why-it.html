Why ICT?

I want to work in ICT because I am curious about technology and how it can solve real-world problems. Seeing robots at a Nissan factory made me realise how technology can be used for much more than just programming.

How will I achieve this?

I want to develop my programming, data, and problem-solving skills through my studies at HZ, personal projects, and internships. I also want to keep learning Python and explore different areas of ICT.

What am I going to do?

I am studying HBO-ICT at HZ and am particularly interested in data science and AI. I want to learn how to use programming and data to solve practical problems.

My Motivation

When I visited a Nissan car factory in Japan during vocational high school, I was fascinated by the robots inspecting car parts. I had studied programming and software development for a few years, but seeing robots being used in real life made me realise how much more there was to ICT than just writing code.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, I also took a Python course during my summer holiday. I enjoyed figuring out how to solve problems with code, and it reminded me why I was interested in technology in the first place.

When choosing what to study next, I was not completely sure what career I wanted. However, I knew I wanted to continue learning about technology. That is why I chose HBO-ICT at HZ. I am especially interested in data science and AI because I want to understand how they work and how they can be used to solve everyday problems.
