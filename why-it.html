Why ICT?

My motivation, approach, and future direction.

My Golden Circle

Why ICT?

I am fascinated by how technology continues to evolve and how it can be used to solve problems in everyday life. During my three years at a vocational high school majoring in IT, I gained a basic understanding of programming and software development. I also took a Python course during the summer holidays, which allowed me to explore programming further and discover how much I enjoyed it.

In what way?

I will achieve my goal by continuously improving my programming skills, learning Python and data analysis, and working on personal projects that allow me to apply what I learn in practice. I will also take advantage of internships and opportunities at HZ to gain experience and learn from people in the ICT industry.

What will I do?

I am studying HBO-ICT and will soon work as a data scientist who analyses data and develops intelligent solutions to real-world problems, making technology more useful and accessible to people from different backgrounds.

My Story

My goal is to solve real-world problems and make technology more useful to people. To achieve this, I will build a strong foundation in programming and develop my skills in data analysis and problem-solving. During my time at HZ, I want to work on personal projects and gain practical experience through internships to learn how to apply my skills in real-world situations. What makes me different is my curiosity about technology and my willingness to keep learning. Having grown up alongside rapid technological development, I have become interested in how AI can help people solve problems in their daily lives. I want to turn this interest into practical skills and eventually use technology to develop solutions that make a positive difference. Although I still have a lot to learn, I am determined to keep improving and work towards a career in ICT.
