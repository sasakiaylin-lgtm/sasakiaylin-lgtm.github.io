My Mission

This year, my mission is to improve my programming skills and become more confident in solving difficult problems. I want to use my determination to keep going when things get challenging instead of giving up. At the same time, I want to manage my perfectionism by focusing on finishing my work on time rather than spending too much time on small details. By the end of the year, I hope to become a more confident, efficient, and skilled ICT student.

My SMART Goals

Improve My Programming Skills

I want to use my determination to become better at programming.

Specific: I will keep working on programming problems even when I find them difficult.

Measurable: I will solve at least two challenging programming exercises every week.

Achievable: I can use exercises from class and ask for help when I get stuck.

Relevant: Practising regularly will help me improve my programming skills.

Time-bound: I will follow this goal for the next three months.

Manage My Perfectionism

I want to spend less time focusing on small details and become better at finishing my work on time.

Specific: I will set time limits for assignments and avoid over-focusing on details.

Measurable: I will finish my assignments within the planned time in at least four out of five assignments.

Achievable: I can set realistic time limits and move on when the important parts are completed.

Relevant: Better time management will help me complete coursework more efficiently.

Time-bound: I will work on this goal for the next three months.
