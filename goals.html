My Mission

As an HBO-ICT student, I am determined to develop my programming skills and become a more confident problem-solver. I will build on my existing knowledge by practising coding regularly and learning from my mistakes instead of giving up when I face challenges. This year, I am also working on managing my stress by staying physically active, maintaining a positive mindset, and stepping outside my comfort zone. My goal is to strengthen my programming foundations and develop the confidence and resilience I need to overcome challenges by the end of my first academic year at HZ.

My SWOT Analysis

Strengths Weaknesses
  • Programming foundation: attended a vocational high school for three years, majoring in IT, where I gained foundational knowledge of programming and software development.
  • Adaptable: up during a period of rapid technological development has made me familiar with continuous change. I believe I can adapt to new technologies and adjust to changes in my environment.
  • Stage fright: find it difficult to speak in front of large groups of people or be the centre of attention. This can make it challenging for me to express my ideas confidently during presentations.
  • Anxiety and overthinking: sometimes overthink situations and feel anxious about stepping outside my comfort zone. This can prevent me from taking opportunities that could help me grow personally and professionally.
Opportunities Threats
  • Internships and minors: and minor programmes can help me connect with professionals in the ICT industry and gain practical experience. They can also give me a better understanding of how technology companies operate and help me explore my career interests.
  • Support from teachers and peers: and fellow students can provide guidance, feedback, and different perspectives. Through group projects, I can learn from others, improve my teamwork skills, and develop my technical knowledge.
  • Dutch language: hope to build my career and settle in the Netherlands. However, limited Dutch language skills could make it more difficult to find certain job opportunities and communicate in workplaces where Dutch is required.
  • Competitive ICT industry: ICT industry is competitive, and many people are developing technical skills and entering the field. To stand out, I will need to continue improving my programming abilities, gain practical experience, and develop skills that are valuable to employers.

My SMART Goals

Improve My Programming Skills

I will continue building on my basic programming knowledge and improve my coding skills through regular practice this semester.

Specific/Measurable: I will practise coding for at least three hours a week and complete at least two programming exercises to strengthen my understanding and become more confident in solving problems independently.

Time-bound: By the end of my first year at HZ, I will have strengthened my programming foundations by practising regularly and solving coding problems more independently.

Manage My Stress

I will reduce my stress by staying physically active, maintaining a positive mindset, and stepping outside my comfort zone more often during my first year at HZ.

Specific/Measurable: I will do physical activities at least twice a week, practise positive thinking when facing challenges, and take at least one opportunity each month that pushes me outside my comfort zone.

Time-bound: I will practise these habits throughout my first year at HZ and evaluate my progress by the end of the academic year.
