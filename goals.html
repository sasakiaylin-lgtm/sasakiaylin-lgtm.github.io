My Mission

This year, my mission is to improve my programming skills and become more confident in solving difficult problems. I want to use my determination to keep going when things get challenging instead of giving up. At the same time, I want to manage my perfectionism by focusing on finishing my work on time rather than spending too much time on small details. By the end of the year, I hope to become a more confident, efficient, and skilled ICT student.
