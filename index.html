HI! I'm Airin

HZ ICT student year 1

I believe ICT suits me because I enjoy solving problems and improving my skills through practice. When I encounter a programming problem that I cannot solve immediately, I search for examples and information to help me understand possible solutions. I find it satisfying to overcome a difficult problem and use what I have learned to solve similar problems independently. I also enjoy comparing different options before making decisions. Whether I am choosing a product or organizing my time and budget, I consider different factors and try to find the most suitable solution. I believe this way of thinking is useful in ICT, where problems can have several possible solutions. Another reason I am interested in this field is the opportunity to keep learning. I enjoy getting better at things that were difficult for me before, and I want to continue developing my technical skills throughout my studies. Through the ICT programme, I hope to strengthen my programming and problem-solving abilities while exploring different areas of technology. I believe ICT suits me because it allows me to approach challenges logically, learn from difficulties, and become more independent in solving problems.
