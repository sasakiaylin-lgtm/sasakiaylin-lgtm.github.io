The ICT Field of Work

Exploring careers, skills, and opportunities in ICT.

What Is the ICT Field?

The ICT field involves developing, managing, and improving technology. ICT professionals work with software, networks, databases, cybersecurity, and many other areas. Technology is used in almost every industry, which creates many different career opportunities.

Different Career Paths

Software Developer

Develops applications and software to solve problems and make tasks easier.

Data Scientist

Analyses data to discover patterns and help organisations make better decisions.

Cybersecurity Specialist

Protects computer systems, networks, and information from security threats.

Skills Needed in ICT

  • Problem-solving and logical thinking
  • Programming and technical knowledge
  • Communication and teamwork
  • Willingness to keep learning
