About me

My name is Aylin, and I was born in a small city called Utsunomiya in Tochigi Prefecture, Japan. I am a future-oriented person who likes to plan ahead and have a clear idea of what I want my future to look like. Choosing ICT gives me peace of mind because I see it as a field with promising career opportunities. I grew up in an era when technology was constantly evolving, allowing me to witness and experience many technological advancements. Thanks to technologies such as AI, I have seen how people of different ages rely on them to find answers and solve problems in a matter of seconds. This has made me even more interested in the potential of technology and its impact on our lives. I am eager to contribute to this fascinating field and gain the knowledge and skills needed to help improve and develop technology further.
