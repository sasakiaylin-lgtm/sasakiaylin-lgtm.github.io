My Chosen Specialisation

Data Science and AI

I am particularly interested in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence because I want to understand how data and technology can be used to solve problems. I want to learn how to analyse data, identify patterns, and develop useful solutions. During my studies, I hope to discover which area best matches my interests and skills.

Future Me — Four Years from Now

In four years, I hope to have completed my ICT degree and gained practical experience through internships and projects. I can see myself working as a Data Scientist or in an AI-related role at a technology company. I want to develop my skills in programming, data analysis, statistics, and problem-solving. I also want to learn how to communicate technical ideas clearly and collaborate effectively with others. My goal is to find a job where I can keep learning and use technology to solve real-world problems.
