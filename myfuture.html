My Direction

My chosen specialisation and future goals.

My Chosen Specialisation

Data Science

I am particularly interested in Data Science because I want to understand how data and technology can be used to solve problems. I want to learn how to analyse data, identify patterns, and develop useful solutions. During my studies, I hope to discover which area best matches my interests and skills.

Future Me — Four Years from Now

In four years, I hope to see myself working towards a career in Data Science, doing something I genuinely enjoy and feel passionate about. Since I grew up watching technology evolve and seeing how people increasingly rely on AI to solve problems, I want to understand how these technologies work behind the scenes and how I can contribute to their development. By then, I hope to have completed my ICT degree at HZ, gained practical experience through internships, and developed my programming and data analysis skills. I know I still have a lot to learn, but I want to become more confident in my abilities and challenge myself to solve problems that once seemed difficult. Ultimately, I hope to build a career where I can keep learning, explore new technologies, and use data to make a meaningful difference in people's lives.
