My Direction

My chosen specialisation and future goals.

My Chosen Specialisation

Data Science

I believe data science suits me because, in my everyday life, I am an organized person who likes to have structure and control over things. I enjoy planning for my future and creating clear steps to achieve my goals. I think this way of thinking connects well with data science because data scientists also need to organize information, analyze situations, and find logical ways to solve problems. What interests me about data science is that I can use programming and data to understand patterns, predict possible outcomes, and make better decisions. For example, just as I create a structured plan to achieve my personal goals, a data scientist uses data to understand a problem and develop a strategy based on evidence rather than guessing. I know I still have a lot to learn, especially in programming and mathematics, but I enjoy improving my skills and challenging myself. I see data science as a direction where I can combine my organized way of thinking with technology to solve real-world problems.

Future Me — Four Years from Now

In four years, I see myself graduating from HZ University of Applied Sciences with a degree in ICT, specializing in Data Science. I hope to have developed strong programming, analytical, and problem-solving skills and gained practical experience through internships or projects. I imagine myself working as a junior data scientist in an international company in Europe, where I can use data to understand problems, discover patterns, and help people make better decisions. I want to work in an environment where I can keep learning, take on new challenges, and grow professionally. Because I am an organized person who likes to plan ahead and work towards clear goals, I want to apply these qualities to my career. I see myself approaching complex problems step by step, using data and evidence to find logical solutions rather than relying on guesses. I know that reaching this goal will require hard work, especially in programming and mathematics. However, I want to use the next four years to build my skills, gain confidence, and become someone who can turn information into meaningful solutions.
